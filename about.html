How We Started

A Studio Built Around Everyday Sparkle

Diva Jewels began with a simple frustration: fine jewelry was either too delicate to wear daily, or too formal to reach for on an ordinary Tuesday. We wanted pieces that could do both — dress up a plain outfit and survive a full day of wear.

Every design starts as a sketch on paper before it's sampled, tested and refined. We work in small batches rather than mass production, which means a little more waiting, but a lot more care in every piece that reaches you.
